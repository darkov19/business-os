# Start Business Workflow Map

Use:

- `business-os-workflow-start-from-scratch.md` as the main process
- `business-os-question-bank.md` for question selection
- `business-os-evidence-rubric.md` for confidence and gate discipline
- `business-os-artifact-templates.md` for output shape

Primary outputs:

- Founder Brief
- Business Definition Note
- Opportunity / Problem Memo
- Customer Research Brief
- Market And Competitor Memo
- Positioning Memo
- Business Concept Dossier

End states:

- ready for deeper planning
- needs validation first
- too vague
- weak or misaligned opportunity
