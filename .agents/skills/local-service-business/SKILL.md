# Local Service Business

Use this skill for local service business work.

## When To Use

Use this skill when the user:

- wants to start or launch a local service business
- needs help with territory, scheduling, dispatch, service recovery, or local acquisition
- is running a geography-bound service and wants a stronger operating model

Do not use this skill when:

- the offer is not geographically constrained
- the business is mainly digital or remote
- the request is only a narrow marketing fix for an already stable local business

## Core Workflow

Run the `Local Service Business` workflow from the canonical Business OS docs.

### Stage Order

1. Local service framing
2. Customer and territory definition
3. Offer and service-delivery design
4. Local economics and capacity review
5. Booking, dispatch, and recovery review
6. Local acquisition and reputation plan
7. Gate and next-step decision

## Required Operating Rules

1. Use the canonical Business OS docs in `/home/darko/Code/chitr/docs`.
2. Keep operations and reputation tightly linked.
3. Do not recommend more demand if scheduling, dispatch, or recovery are still fragile.
4. Prefer narrower territories and tighter delivery standards over premature expansion.
5. When the local-service path is clear, produce an Execution Activation Plan and a 30/60 Day Execution Plan.
6. Use evidence honestly and state confidence clearly.

## Default Output Set

Aim to produce or outline:

- Business Definition Note
- Customer Research Brief
- Offer Architecture Document
- Pricing And Unit Economics Memo
- Local Service Operations Plan
- Route-To-Market Strategy Memo
- Customer Success And Retention Memo where repeat service matters
- Execution Activation Plan where the operating path is clear
- 30/60 Day Execution Plan where launch or tightening work is ready
- Business Concept Dossier or Launch Dossier depending on stage

## Gate Behavior

By the end, classify the local-service path into one of:

- launch narrowly
- tighten operations first
- improve density before expanding
- validate first
