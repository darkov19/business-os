# SaaS Digital Product

Use this skill for SaaS and digital product business work.

## When To Use

Use this skill when the user:

- is building or validating a SaaS or AI product business
- wants help launching a digital product
- needs onboarding, activation, retention, pricing, or SaaS GTM logic
- is asking for software-business guidance rather than generic startup advice

Do not use this skill when:

- the offer is mainly a service with no meaningful software product behavior
- the user only needs a general start-from-scratch workflow
- the request is only a narrow marketing fix for an already stable software business

## Core Workflow

Run the `SaaS / Digital Product` workflow from the canonical Business OS docs.

### Stage Order

1. SaaS framing
2. Customer and product outcome definition
3. Activation and onboarding review
4. Pricing and GTM design
5. Retention and product trust review
6. Validation or launch plan
7. Gate and next-step decision

## Required Operating Rules

1. Use the canonical Business OS docs in `/home/darko/Code/chitr/docs`.
2. Keep a clear distinction between acquisition, activation, retention, and trust.
3. Do not recommend aggressive growth if onboarding, activation, or retention remain weak.
4. Prefer narrow launch or bounded validation over broad software ambition.
5. When the SaaS path is clear, produce an Execution Activation Plan and a 30/60 Day Execution Plan.
6. Use evidence honestly and state confidence clearly.

## Default Output Set

Aim to produce or outline:

- Business Definition Note
- Customer Research Brief
- Offer Architecture Document
- SaaS / Digital Product Growth Note
- Pricing And Unit Economics Memo
- Route-To-Market Strategy Memo
- Customer Success And Retention Memo where relevant
- Execution Activation Plan where the SaaS path is clear
- 30/60 Day Execution Plan where validation or launch work is ready
- Business Concept Dossier or Launch Dossier depending on stage

## Gate Behavior

By the end, classify the SaaS path into one of:

- validate first
- narrow launch
- improve onboarding or activation first
- improve retention or trust first
