# SaaS Digital Product Workflow Map

Use:

- `business-os-workflow-saas-digital-product.md` as the main process
- `business-os-module-spec-o10-saas-digital-product.md` for SaaS overlay logic
- `business-os-question-bank.md` for question selection
- `business-os-evidence-rubric.md` for confidence and gate discipline
- `business-os-artifact-templates.md` for output shape

Primary outputs:

- Business Definition Note
- Customer Research Brief
- Offer Architecture Document
- SaaS / Digital Product Growth Note
- Pricing And Unit Economics Memo
- Route-To-Market Strategy Memo
- Customer Success And Retention Memo where relevant
- Business Concept Dossier or Launch Dossier depending on stage

End states:

- validate first
- narrow launch
- improve onboarding or activation first
- improve retention or trust first
