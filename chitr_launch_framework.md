# Chitr Launch Framework

Author: Viral / Studio Aalekh
Status: Strategic Foundation v2
Purpose: Define the business, brand, product, operations, and launch logic required to build Chitr on a stronger foundation.

---

## 1. Strategic Premise

Chitr should be built as a design-led art brand that sells finished, material-driven wall artworks for modern homes and design-conscious spaces.

The business is not trying to compete with:

- low-cost mass decor
- open-edition print marketplaces
- fine-art gallery representation

Chitr should sit in the space between premium home decor and collectible design objects.

That means the business must be built around:

- distinctive material language
- strong point of view
- controlled product architecture
- premium presentation
- repeatable production standards

---

## 2. Positioning

### Core Positioning

Chitr creates tactile, design-forward wall art for buyers who want more character than mass decor and more accessibility than gallery art.

### Core Promise

Every Chitr piece should feel:

- intentional
- finished
- premium
- installation-ready
- visually calm but materially rich

### Target Customer

Primary customers:

- design-conscious homeowners
- apartment owners furnishing premium spaces
- interior designers sourcing statement-ready wall art
- buyers looking for elevated gifting or one-time investment decor pieces

### Non-Goals

Chitr should not optimize for:

- high-SKU customization at launch
- trend-driven fast catalog rotation
- low-price volume strategy
- marketplace-led brand building

---

## 3. Launch Recommendation

The original idea of launching 25-27 artworks across many material categories is creatively ambitious but operationally heavy for a first launch.

### Recommended Launch Scope

Launch with 12-18 artworks across 3 core material families:

1. Textured surface works
2. Textile and stitch-led works
3. Layered wood or MDF works

This narrower launch is stronger because it:

- reduces prototyping load
- improves production consistency
- simplifies photography and storytelling
- makes quality control easier
- lets the brand learn from real demand before expanding

### Phase 2 Expansion

Expand into hybrid formats only after launch validation:

- paint plus embroidery
- print plus textile
- more complex multi-material compositions

---

## 4. Product System

### Product Rules

For launch, Chitr should operate with controlled SKUs:

- no customer-facing size options
- no customer-facing color options
- no customer-facing frame options

Each artwork should be sold as a complete designed object.

### Internal Standardization

Internally, Chitr should standardize:

- panel sizes
- hanging systems
- frame construction method
- packaging format
- finishing process

Important clarification:
Frame color can exist as an internal design decision for a specific artwork, but it should not become a customer choice at launch.

### Recommended Formats

- Single statement piece
- Diptych
- Triptych
- Cluster set

Asymmetrical layouts can be added later after packaging and installation complexity is understood.

---

## 5. Art Direction

Chitr should feel calm, tactile, architectural, and crafted.

### Recommended Visual Territories

- Organic minimal
- Textural landscape
- Architectural geometry
- Material abstraction
- Modern craft

### Color Direction

Base palette:

- sand
- clay
- off-white
- charcoal

Accent palette:

- ochre
- rust
- olive
- indigo

Use accent color sparingly. The main signature should come from texture, layering, and material contrast rather than bright color.

---

## 6. Operating Model

### Launch Production Model

Recommended starting model:

- made to order by default
- limited ready stock only for hero or best-selling pieces after validation

This protects cash flow and reduces dead inventory risk.

### Production Principles

- fixed materials list
- repeatable process by category
- quality checklist before packing
- documented build spec for every artwork

---

## 7. Pricing Principles

Pricing should not be set only by aesthetic judgment.

Every SKU should be priced using:

- bill of materials
- labor time
- packaging cost
- shipping assumption
- overhead allocation
- marketing and platform cost
- target gross margin

Premium positioning only works if the margin structure supports content creation, packaging quality, and growth.

---

## 8. Commerce and Launch Principles

The website should help customers understand three things quickly:

1. what Chitr is
2. why it feels premium
3. why the price is justified

Each product should therefore include:

- clean front-facing view
- styled room context
- close detail shots
- scale clarity
- craftsmanship explanation
- material description
- production lead time

---

## 9. Custom Orders

Custom work should remain a separate service, not a variant system inside the standard catalog.

This keeps the core business scalable while preserving a premium consultation channel.

Recommended custom channels:

- inquiry form
- designer consultation
- project-based quote

## 11. Immediate Priority

The next stage should not be "make more ideas." It should be "make the business logic tighter."

Immediate priorities:

1. confirm target customer and buying context
2. define launch collection scope and SKU count
3. build costing model for every product family
4. document production workflow and QC
5. define website structure and content requirements
6. identify open assumptions that need validation

If these are strong, the brand will have a much better foundation to grow.
