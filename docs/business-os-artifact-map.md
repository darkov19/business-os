## 1. Artifact Philosophy

The Business OS should produce:

- durable documents
- decision records
- operating guides
- control trackers

It should not produce:

- generic business-plan filler
- polished certainty when evidence is weak

Version 2 expands the artifact system so the OS captures not only strategy and launch, but also:

- financial planning
- retention and support
- experimentation
- software / digital-product specifics
- local service specifics
- team and org decisions

## 6. New Master Dossier Expectations

Version 2 does not require entirely new top-level dossier types, but it expands what existing dossiers should contain.

### Business Master Dossier

Should now also include where relevant:

- financial planning summary
- retention / support summary
- experiment backlog summary
- org and team notes

### Launch Dossier

Should now also include where relevant:

- support and issue-resolution logic
- working-capital or cash-pressure note
- pilot logic if launch is phased
- activation plan summary
- 30/60 day execution plan summary

### Expansion Decision Dossier

Should now also include where relevant:

- financial strain assessment
- experiment design
- support / delivery burden
- activation plan summary if expansion is approved or piloted

## 7. Intent To Artifact Updates

### Start From Scratch

Primary outputs:

- Founder Brief
- Business Definition Note
- Opportunity / Problem Memo
- Business Concept Dossier
- Execution Activation Plan where a first route-to-market direction is agreed
- Assumption Log

### Validate An Idea

Primary outputs:

- Validation Memo
- Evidence Tracker
- Experiment / Pilot Design Memo when testing is required

### Pivot Existing Business

Primary outputs:

- Current State / Pivot Diagnosis Memo
- Asset And Capability Audit
- Pivot Strategy Dossier
- Financial Planning Memo when the pivot affects cash structure
- Execution Activation Plan
- 30/60 Day Execution Plan where the pivot should move into a pilot or phased rollout

### Launch A New Product Or Service

Primary outputs:

- Offer Architecture Document
- Pricing And Unit Economics Memo
- Route-To-Market Strategy Memo
- Marketing Strategy Plan
- Launch Readiness Document
- Execution Activation Plan
- 30/60 Day Execution Plan
- Premium D2C Launch Commerce Note where relevant
- Launch Dossier
- Support And Issue-Resolution Playbook where launch support matters

### Improve Sales Or Marketing

Primary outputs:

- Route-To-Market Strategy Memo
- Marketing Strategy Plan
- Brand, Messaging, And Trust Framework
- Execution Activation Plan
- Marketplace / Platform Strategy Memo if relevant

### Fix Pricing, Economics, Or Capital Pressure

Primary outputs:

- Pricing And Unit Economics Memo
- Financial Planning Memo
- Capital Allocation Priorities Note

### Improve Operations Or Delivery

Primary outputs:

- Operations Playbook
- Inventory / Fulfillment Assessment or Local Service Operations Plan depending on business type

### Improve Retention, Support, Or Post-Sale Experience

Primary outputs:

- Customer Success And Retention Memo
- Support And Issue-Resolution Playbook

### Evaluate Expansion / New Category

Primary outputs:

- Expansion Smoke Test Memo
- Experiment / Pilot Design Memo
- Financial Planning Memo if the expansion affects capital burden
- Execution Activation Plan when the answer is add now or pilot first
- Expansion Decision Dossier

### Document My Business

Primary outputs:

- Business Master Dossier
- Artifact Index
- Open Questions Register

### Diagnose What Is Broken

Primary outputs:

- Current State / Pivot Diagnosis Memo
- Decision Gate Tracker
- diagnosis memo and repair plan

## 8. Chitr In The Current System

For Chitr by Aalekh, the current artifact system improves the most in three places:

- financial planning around launch and future expansion
- pilot / experiment logic for new categories
- post-sale and support logic once product commerce begins

That makes the OS more operationally realistic without changing the core Chitr architecture.
