## 1. What The Business OS Is

The Business OS is a modular, AI-friendly operating system for helping users:

- start a business from scratch
- validate ideas
- pivot existing businesses
- launch new products or services
- improve growth, economics, operations, or retention
- evaluate expansion opportunities
- generate durable business documentation

It is designed to adapt to the user's query rather than force every user through a single path.

---

## 2. What Changed From The Archived Draft Set

Version 1 proved the architecture on Chitr and similar businesses.

Version 2 expands the system to cover:

- deeper finance beyond pricing
- post-sale and retention logic
- SaaS / digital product businesses
- local service businesses
- education / coaching businesses
- marketplace and platform dependence
- experimentation and pilot design
- organization and team design

The architecture remains the same.

The coverage inside the architecture is broader.

## 9. Decision Gates

The OS continues to use explicit gate outcomes:

1. Proceed
2. Proceed With Warnings
3. Conditional Proceed
4. Defer
5. Block / Validate First

Version 2 strengthens gate logic for:

- working-capital pressure
- retention and support readiness
- SaaS activation and reliability
- local-service readiness
- pilot design quality
