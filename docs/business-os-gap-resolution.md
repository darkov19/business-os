## 1. Resolved In The Current System

The following gaps identified in the first stress test are now addressed architecturally.

### New Core Modules Added

- M17 Financial Planning And Capital Allocation
- M18 Customer Success And Retention System

### New Overlays Added

- O10 SaaS / Digital Product Overlay
- O11 Local Service Operations Overlay
- O12 Education / Coaching Overlay
- O13 Marketplace / Platform Strategy Overlay
- O14 Experimentation And Pilot Design Overlay
- O15 Organization And Team Design Overlay

### Existing Modules Strengthened

- M12 Route-To-Market And Sales Architecture
- M13 Marketing And Growth System
- M15 Legal, Finance, Risk, And Setup

### Artifact System Expanded

The artifact map now includes outputs for:

- financial planning
- capital allocation
- retention and support
- marketplace strategy
- experiments and pilots
- local service operations
- education delivery
- org and team design

### Gate System Strengthened

The decision-gate system now accounts more directly for:

- financial resilience
- working-capital strain
- post-sale sustainability
- activation quality
- local-service execution burden

---

## 2. Improved But Not Fully Solved

These areas are now represented, but will still need deeper implementation detail in templates or overlays.

- SaaS / digital product depth
- local service operational depth
- education / coaching operating depth
- marketplace execution detail
- org design and delegation detail

These are no longer architecture gaps.
They are now implementation-depth gaps.

---

## 3. Still Open After The Current Revision

The following areas are still not deeply handled and may require later overlays or specialist modules.

- regulated industries
- healthcare-specific operations
- finance-specific regulation-heavy businesses
- franchise systems
- deep manufacturing operations
- multi-country compliance-heavy businesses

These are acceptable exclusions for the current system.

---

## 4. Conclusion

Version 2 resolves the most important structural weaknesses found in version 1.

The Business OS is now much more credible as a reusable system across:

- Chitr-like pivots
- physical product brands
- local service businesses
- SaaS / AI businesses
- education / coaching businesses
- channel and expansion-heavy businesses

The next risk is no longer missing architecture.

The next risk is weak implementation detail.
