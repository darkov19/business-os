# Business OS Scope v1

Status: Draft for discussion
Purpose: Define the scope, structure, and operating principles for an AI-assisted Business Operating System that helps users create strong business documentation and make better business decisions from scratch, during pivots, and during expansion.

---

## 1. Why This Exists

Most founders do not fail because they have no ideas.

They fail because:

- they start with vague assumptions
- they skip customer and market clarity
- they confuse activity with progress
- they do not understand pricing, channels, or operations deeply enough
- they create scattered notes instead of decision-ready documents

This Business OS should solve that problem.

It should help a user move from:

- vague idea
- partial business understanding
- messy notes
- unsupported assumptions

to:

- structured thinking
- evidence-backed decisions
- clear business documentation
- stronger launch and operating plans

---

## 2. Core Goal

Build a modular, AI-friendly Business Operating System that can:

- adapt to the user's query and business stage
- ask the right questions before generating advice
- identify what needs research
- guide structured business decision-making
- produce usable business documents
- support future AI skills and workflows

---

## 3. Primary Use Cases

The system should support users who want to:

1. start a new business from scratch
2. validate an early business idea
3. pivot an existing business
4. launch a new product or service line
5. improve sales or marketing
6. fix pricing or unit economics
7. improve operations or delivery systems
8. evaluate a new category, expansion, or adjacency
9. generate strong business documentation
10. diagnose why a business is stuck

---

## 4. Design Principles

### 4.1 Adaptive, Not Static

The system should adapt to the user's intent, rather than forcing everyone through the same sequence.

### 4.2 Ask Before Telling

The system should gather context and ask clarifying questions before generating strategic outputs.

### 4.3 Evidence Over Assumption

The system should distinguish clearly between:

- known facts
- founder assumptions
- unverified beliefs
- validated insights

### 4.4 Modular By Decision Area

The underlying OS should be organized around decision areas such as customer, offer, pricing, operations, and channels.

### 4.5 Guided By Entry Workflow

Users should still be able to enter through intuitive workflows such as:

- start from scratch
- pivot
- launch
- expand
- fix a problem

### 4.6 Reusable Across Business Types

The system should work across different business models, while allowing overlays for business-specific logic.

### 4.7 AI-Agent Compatible

Each module should be structured so coding agents or business-oriented AI skills can run it reliably.

### 4.8 Document-Centered

The OS should produce durable documents and decision records, not only conversational advice.

---

## 5. Non-Goals

This system should not:

- act like a generic motivational startup guide
- produce shallow business plans from thin inputs
- pretend all businesses follow the same path
- replace legal or financial professionals where specialized advice is required
- recommend scaling before fundamentals are validated
- generate polished outputs without surfacing uncertainty

---

## 6. Top-Level Architecture

The Business OS should have six layers.

### 6.1 Intent Layer

Determines what the user is trying to do.

Examples:

- start
- validate
- pivot
- launch
- improve
- expand
- diagnose
- document

### 6.2 Context Layer

Determines what kind of business the user is working on.

Examples:

- physical product
- service business
- studio or agency
- ecommerce brand
- B2B supplier
- distributor model
- hybrid business

### 6.3 Discovery Layer

Defines the minimum questions needed before useful outputs can be produced.

### 6.4 Decision Module Layer

Contains the core business modules such as customer, offer, pricing, operations, and channels.

### 6.5 Artifact Layer

Defines what documents, trackers, or decision memos the system should generate.

### 6.6 Agent Execution Layer

Defines how AI agents should run the workflows, ask questions, perform research, and decide when to pause or escalate uncertainty.

## 8. Business-Type Overlays

The core OS should remain reusable, but it needs overlays for business-specific realities.

Initial overlays should include:

- physical product business
- service business
- studio / creative business
- ecommerce-led business
- B2B / project-led business
- distributor or channel-led business
- import / sourcing-heavy business

Later overlays may include:

- SaaS / AI products
- education businesses
- franchise or multi-location models

---

## 9. Entry Workflows

The user should not have to understand the whole OS before getting help.

The system should offer entry workflows such as:

1. Start From Scratch
2. Validate An Idea
3. Pivot Existing Business
4. Launch A New Product Or Service
5. Improve Sales Or Marketing
6. Fix Pricing Or Economics
7. Improve Operations
8. Evaluate Expansion
9. Document My Business
10. Diagnose What Is Broken

Each workflow should route into the right decision modules.

---

## 10. Module Spec Format

Each module should follow a consistent internal structure so AI agents can use it reliably.

Every module should define:

- purpose
- when to use it
- prerequisites
- questions to ask the user
- external research to perform
- evidence required
- assumptions to log
- outputs to create
- red flags
- decision gate
- next possible modules

---

The OS should generate durable outputs, not only chat responses.

Possible outputs include:

- founder brief
- business concept note
- customer research brief
- market research memo
- positioning memo
- offer architecture doc
- productization assessment
- pricing and unit economics sheet
- channel strategy memo
- marketing plan
- launch readiness doc
- operations playbook
- expansion smoke test memo

## 12. Research Philosophy

The system should use research deliberately, not as filler.

Research should come from a mix of:

- business guides
- practical books and frameworks
- industry reports
- competitor websites
- founder interviews
- operating benchmarks
- user interviews

The system should prefer research that improves decisions, not research that only sounds intelligent.

## 13. AI Agent Behavior Rules

The future skills and agents built on this OS should behave with the following rules.

### 13.1 Ask Necessary Questions

If important information is missing, the agent should ask targeted questions before drafting major outputs.

### 13.2 Do Not Over-Assume

The agent should mark assumptions clearly and avoid presenting guesses as conclusions.

### 13.3 Separate Facts From Inference

The agent should distinguish:

- user-provided facts
- researched facts
- inferred conclusions
- open questions

### 13.4 Gate Major Decisions

The agent should not recommend major moves without minimum evidence.

Examples:

- do not recommend distributors before margin and fulfillment readiness are proven
- do not recommend scale before repeatability is proven
- do not recommend paid growth before message and offer clarity exist

### 13.5 Produce Structured Outputs

The agent should convert conversations into usable business documents.

### 13.6 Adapt To User Maturity

If the user is early-stage, the agent should guide more.

If the user is advanced, the agent should act more like a structured strategist.

### 13.7 Preserve Reusability

The agent should avoid embedding business-specific assumptions into the universal core unless they belong in an overlay.

---

## 14. Open Questions For Scope v1

These decisions still need to be settled before the full framework is written.

1. Should v1 be optimized first for solo founders and very small teams?
2. Should legal and compliance remain country-neutral in the core, with India overlays later?
3. Should modules block progress when evidence is weak, or allow draft outputs with warnings?
4. Which business types should be first-class in v1?
5. How deep should execution playbooks go in v1 versus later expansions?

---

## 15. Recommended Next Step

Do not write the full framework yet.

First create:

1. intent map
2. context classifier
3. module map
4. artifact map
5. decision-gate philosophy

Once those are agreed, the full Business OS framework can be written with much better structure and less drift.
